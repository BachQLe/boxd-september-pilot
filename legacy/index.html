What we will do

One connected growth system.

Not just content. We build the path from discovery to purchase, instrument it, and improve it every week.

01

Audit the journey

Map how UVA students currently discover Box’d, decide what to order, and reach checkout. Identify the biggest points of friction.

02

Create the content

Plan and produce three distinct organic content sets, built around rational proof, social proof, and product utility.

03

Build conversion pages

Give every campaign a focused destination with one clear action instead of sending every viewer to the same generic page.

04

Connect Toast

Route orders to Box’d-branded Toast Online Ordering for pickup or delivery, with campaign links and offer codes carried into the path.

05

Measure intent

Track landing views, menu selections, order clicks, chooser results, and promo redemptions. Marketplace links remain click-level only.

06

Optimize weekly

Review performance, identify the strongest hooks and offers, then shift the next week’s creative and landing-page emphasis.
